01 About Me

I am a third year Electronics and Computer Engineering student interested in machine learning, computer vision, and embedded systems. I enjoy building systems that combine software and hardware to solve practical problems.

Most of my projects focus on computer vision models and embedded devices. I have worked on training models for tasks like helmet detection and plant disease classification using PyTorch, and on hardware projects such as an RFID based attendance system and assistive smart glasses that read printed text aloud using OCR and text to speech.

My work usually involves the full pipeline of a project, from data preparation and model training to integrating the solution with embedded hardware such as ESP32 or Raspberry Pi. I am particularly interested in systems where machine learning interacts with the physical world through sensors and edge devices.

Currently I am exploring deeper topics in computer vision, efficient ML models, and low level systems programming.
